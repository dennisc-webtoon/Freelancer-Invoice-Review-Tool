Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded. "Your team" is derived from historical approvals: a freelancer is on Dennis Choi's team if Dennis has ever approved one of their past invoices. This works because "new" invoices haven't been approved yet and have no approver assigned. The chronology check walks through every invoice for each freelancer × series combination (across the full history in the CSV) and flags gaps, overlaps, and out-of-order submissions. The rate check compares each pending invoice's per-episode rate against the most-frequent rate across that freelancer's prior PAID invoices for the same series; Sam Kim and Hyerim Ko are excluded because they bill by hour and character count. The Airtable check matches each invoice episode by translator email + series name + episode number, then compares the relevant due date (Translation for "Freelance Translator" tag, Lettering for "Freelance Translator/GD" and "Mantra" tags) against the invoice's work month.
